I’m a front-end developer focused on building and evolving complex web applications, working at the intersection of modern frontend architecture and developer experience. I simply love to code. I’m curious by nature and interested in a wide range of topics—from history, music, and art to questions about consciousness.

A recurring theme in my work is helping developers understand large systems. I’m particularly interested in tools and workflows that make complex repositories easier to navigate. Recently I’ve been exploring AI-assisted development, agent-driven tooling, and ways LLMs can analyze architecture, surface patterns, and accelerate onboarding. I’m also passionate about UX and improving the collaboration loop between design and engineering.

Alongside my main work, I’m involved in an early-stage project building a Next.js application for searching large photo libraries, experimenting with pragmatic startup engineering practices and modern web tooling.

Outside of engineering, I spend time with film, music, and language learning. I’m also interested in Buddhist philosophy and its perspective on attention and clarity of mind. I enjoy the sea whenever possible, and as a father I often find myself turning history and science into stories that can compete with Minecraft and YouTube.

I’m always interested in conversations about frontend architecture, developer tooling, UX, and thoughtful uses of AI in software development.
